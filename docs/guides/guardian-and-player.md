# Guardian and player guide

For parents and carers of a player, and for players themselves. This is where you
follow a child's progress and fees, get told what's happening, and say yes or no
when the team wants to enter a tournament.

_Last checked: 2026-09-21 · Try it: sign in at `/demo` as **Guardian** (Mylene
Bautista) or **Player** (Angelica Alvarado, the same player). The demo buttons take
you straight to your page; a normal sign-in doesn't, see the note below._

## Get your account

Your club adds you first. You can't sign up on your own.

1. **The club adds you as a guardian** on your child's profile, with your name,
   phone and **email**, and chooses **Invite**.
2. **The club tells you.** Inviting doesn't send an email or a message, so expect a
   phone call or a note from the club.
3. **You create your account.** Go to `/guardian-signup` and use **the same email**
   the club has on file. Choose a password. Your account links to the invite by
   itself. If the page says to check your email, do that first; the link finishes
   the sign-up.
4. You land on **your page** at `/guardian`.

If you sign in later and the club hasn't linked you, you'll see *No guardian
account*, or *No children linked to your account yet — ask your club to link you to
your player.* The club needs to link you.

### Finding your page again (read this)

**After a normal sign-in you land on the Dulà HQ homepage, and there is no link
from there to your page.** Your page is its own address:

- Guardians: `/guardian`
- Players: `/player`

**Bookmark it.** Notifications also link straight to it. Until the app adds a link,
this is the way in.

## Your page as a guardian

The top of the page says *Hi, [your name]* and how many children you have. Below it
are tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **[Your child's name]** | One tab per child. Their profile, described below |
| **Tournaments** | Requests to confirm or decline your child's tournament place |
| **Billing** | Invoices you can pay, and a form to report a payment |
| **Announcements** | Messages from the club |

### Your child's profile

Each child has six tabs. What's inside depends on what the club has allowed you to
see.

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Position, team, attendance, active goals, upcoming sessions, recent activity |
| **Development** | Goals, evaluations, and coach feedback marked for the family. Private coach notes are never shown to you |
| **Fees** | The charges the club has made and what's been paid |
| **Membership** | Registration status, and which teams they've been on |
| **Documents** | Which forms are in, and whether each is approved. Only the club uploads documents |
| **Family** | The guardians on file and the child's own login |

You only ever see **your own child**. Other players on the team, and other
families' fees, are invisible to you.

### Confirm or decline a tournament place

When a coach puts your child on a tournament roster, you're asked first: players
under 18 can't be entered without a guardian's yes.

1. Open the **Tournaments** tab. Each request shows the tournament and the child.
   A badge on the tab counts the ones waiting for you.
2. Choose **Confirm participation** to say yes.
3. To say no, choose **Decline**, write **why** (the box says *Let the coach know
   why*, and it's required), and choose **Confirm decline**.

If you decline, the coach is notified straight away. If you confirm, they see it the
next time they open the roster. A request that nobody answers eventually expires,
so don't leave it. If you confirm and then can't attend, tell the coach: the roster
is theirs to change.

### Pay an invoice

The **Billing** tab lists invoices from the club that can be paid by QR or bank
transfer. Each shows how much has been paid of the total.

1. Read the payment instructions shown on the invoice (a QR code or account
   details).
2. Pay the club outside the app.
3. In the **Manual payment** form, enter the **Amount**, choose the **Method** (QR
   transfer, Bank transfer, Cash, or Other), type the **Reference number** from
   your receipt, and add a **Note** if useful.
4. Choose **Submit payment for verification**.

The club checks that the money arrived and either accepts it or rejects it with a
reason. If it's rejected, the invoice reopens so you can try again.

Payments are **not taken in the app**. Verification is manual, so allow the club
time.

**Fee charges** that the club records against your child (registration, monthly
dues) appear under **Fees** on the child's tab. Those are usually paid to the club
directly, and the club then records the payment. If you've paid and it isn't
showing, ask the club to record it.

## Notifications

Choose **Enable** on the **Turn on notifications** banner to get alerts on this
device, even when the app is closed. If your browser has blocked notifications the
banner says so; enable them in your browser's settings for this site.

The **bell** at the top of every page keeps a list of everything sent to you, with
a count of what's unread. Choose one to open it, or **Mark all read**.

You're notified when:

- a fee is charged, a payment is recorded, or a charge's status changes;
- your child gets a goal, evaluation or coach note that the family can see;
- their membership status changes, or they're moved to another team;
- a document is uploaded or reviewed;
- a tournament place needs your answer, or a roster is finalized;
- they're added to or removed from a trip.

Notifications are shown in the app and on your device. **Nothing is emailed.**

## Your page as a player

Players don't sign up themselves. A club member or your guardian links an existing
login to your profile using its email address. Until then you'll see *No player
account linked*.

Once linked, `/player` shows **your own** profile: the same six tabs as above
(**Overview, Development, Fees, Membership, Documents, Family**) plus
**Announcements**. It is read-only, you only ever see your own information, and
players 18 and over get the notifications themselves.

## When something doesn't work

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| *No guardian account* | Your login isn't linked to a guardian record. Ask the club to add you and invite you |
| *No children linked…* | You have an account, but no child is attached. Ask the club to link you |
| You can't sign up | Use the exact email the club entered. Ask the club to check it and to choose **Invite** |
| You signed in and see a list of clubs | That's the public homepage. Go to `/guardian` (or `/player`) |
| A tab is empty | Either nothing has happened yet, or the club hasn't allowed you to see it |
| A payment shows "submitted" for days | The club hasn't verified it yet. Ask them |
